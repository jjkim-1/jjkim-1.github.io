Nonlinear 6-DOF Flight Dynamics & LQR Control

A MATLAB-based nonlinear six degree of freedom flight dynamics and control simulation for a notional aerospace vehicle. The project combines quaternion attitude dynamics, steady flight trim, numerical linearization, modal and controllability analysis, LQR state feedback design, and nonlinear closed-loop validation.

Modeling and Control Formulation

6-DOF Equations of Motion

The nonlinear plant contains body frame translational dynamics, rigid body rotational dynamics, navigation frame position propagation, and quaternion attitude propagation.

Translational Dynamics

\[ \dot{\mathbf V}_b = \frac{\mathbf F_b}{m} - \boldsymbol{\omega}_b \times \mathbf V_b + C_{nb}\mathbf g_n \]

where

\[ \mathbf V_b = \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ w \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \boldsymbol{\omega}_b = \begin{bmatrix} p \\ q \\ r \end{bmatrix} \]

and gravity in the North-East-Down navigation frame is

\[ \mathbf g_n = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ g \end{bmatrix}. \]

Rotational Dynamics

\[ \mathbf I \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_b = \mathbf M_b - \boldsymbol{\omega}_b \times \left( \mathbf I \boldsymbol{\omega}_b \right) \]

or equivalently,

\[ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_b = \mathbf I^{-1} \left[ \mathbf M_b - \boldsymbol{\omega}_b \times \left( \mathbf I \boldsymbol{\omega}_b \right) \right]. \]

Position is propagated in the NED frame using the body to navigation direction cosine matrix:

\[ \dot{\mathbf p}_n = C_{bn}\mathbf V_b \]
\[ \mathbf p_n = \begin{bmatrix} p_N \\ p_E \\ p_D \end{bmatrix}. \]
Quaternion Attitude Representation

A scalar-first unit quaternion represents body to NED orientation:

\[ \mathbf q = \begin{bmatrix} q_0 \\ q_1 \\ q_2 \\ q_3 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \|\mathbf q\| = 1. \]

The complete nonlinear vehicle state is

\[ \mathbf x = \begin{bmatrix} p_N & p_E & p_D & u & v & w & q_0 & q_1 & q_2 & q_3 & p & q & r \end{bmatrix}^{T}. \]

Quaternion propagation is performed using the body angular rates:

\[ \dot{\mathbf q} = \frac{1}{2} \begin{bmatrix} -q_1 p - q_2 q - q_3 r \\[4pt] q_0 p + q_2 r - q_3 q \\[4pt] q_0 q - q_1 r + q_3 p \\[4pt] q_0 r + q_1 q - q_2 p \end{bmatrix}. \]

Quaternion normalization is enforced during intermediate RK4 stages and following each completed integration step to maintain the unit norm constraint.

Trim & Reduced Linearization

A steady flight operating point is solved numerically at approximately 1000 m altitude and 300 m/s airspeed.

The longitudinal trim decision vector is

\[ \mathbf z = \begin{bmatrix} \alpha & \theta & u_{\mathrm{pitch}} & u_{\mathrm{thrust}} \end{bmatrix}^{T}. \]

The quaternion state is replaced locally by a three-component attitude-error coordinate, producing the reduced perturbation state:

\[ \delta\mathbf x_r = \begin{bmatrix} \delta p_N & \delta p_E & \delta p_D & \delta u & \delta v & \delta w & \epsilon_x & \epsilon_y & \epsilon_z & \delta p & \delta q & \delta r \end{bmatrix}^{T}. \]

The local reduced dynamics are represented by the state-space model:

\[ \delta\dot{\mathbf x} = A\delta\mathbf x + B\delta\mathbf u. \]

The attitude error follows the right-multiplicative quaternion convention:

\[ \mathbf q_{\mathrm{current}} = \mathbf q_{\mathrm{reference}} \otimes \mathbf q_{\mathrm{error}} \]

with the local attitude coordinate

\[ \boldsymbol{\epsilon} = 2 \mathbf q_{\mathrm{error},v}. \]
LQR State-Feedback Controller

A continuous time Linear Quadratic Regulator is designed around the reduced trim linearization.

The LQR controller minimizes the quadratic performance index:

\[ J = \int_{0}^{\infty} \left( \delta\mathbf x^{T} Q \delta\mathbf x + \delta\mathbf u^{T} R \delta\mathbf u \right) dt. \]

The resulting perturbation feedback law is

\[ \delta\mathbf u = -K\delta\mathbf x. \]

The command supplied to the nonlinear vehicle model is

\[ \mathbf u_{\mathrm{ctrl}} = \mathbf u_{\mathrm{trim}} + \delta\mathbf u. \]

The LQR gain is therefore designed using the reduced linear model but validated against the original nonlinear quaternion based vehicle plant.
