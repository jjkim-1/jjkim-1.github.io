Purpose and contribution

A measurement pipeline for actuator motion

Soft robotic actuators deform as pressure changes. Tracking a visible marker and its corresponding depth provides an estimate of how that point moves in three dimensions relative to a reference configuration.

I developed and calibrated a camera based measurement pipeline for three dimensional displacement estimation and validation of soft robotic actuators. The pipeline combines marker detection and pairing, depth processing, camera calibration, coordinate transformation, and displacement analysis.

This page presents a local reproduction using the lab project code and recorded images. The figures document the processing workflow and its agreement with an existing displacement record; they do not establish independent measurement accuracy.

Method

From image coordinates to displacement

  1. Detect and pair markers. Color segmentation and geometric filtering identify candidate markers. The configured pairing method associates the reference and endpoint detections.
  2. Evaluate depth. Local depth samples provide a depth estimate and consistency checks for each marker.
  3. Transform coordinates. Camera intrinsics convert pixel coordinates and depth into camera coordinates. The supplied extrinsic transformation maps these into the working coordinate frame.
  4. Calculate displacement. Subtract the reference position from the endpoint position and compare the result with the historical record.

Interpretation

Reproducibility and limitations

The maximum difference between rerun and historical displacement vectors is 1.150 mm at 16 kPa. This is a reproduction difference, not a sensor accuracy specification. Its cause has not been established.

Data provenance and calibration assumptions

The demonstration uses the Actuator_characterization_1007 color and depth images and the workbook’s main displacement record, dated October 7, 2025. The historical text record agrees with that main sheet at its stored precision.

The workbook’s separate exp sheet omits two entries and renumbers later pressures, so it is excluded. Its planar R quantity is not used as a 3D magnitude. The sim sheet is also excluded because its model provenance and scaling are not established.

The rerun retains the configured cam2 selection, although test descriptions say CAM-1. It assumes depth is aligned with color and expressed in millimeters. These calibration and recording assumptions still require confirmation; matching image dimensions alone does not establish alignment.

Processing runs on saved files without ROS. The local runner adapts file paths and disables GUI display while preserving the supplied detection, pairing, depth processing, and coordinate calculations.
