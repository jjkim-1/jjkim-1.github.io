About me

I am a recent USC M.S. Aerospace Engineering graduate with an interdisciplinary background in biochemistry, microbiology, immunology, molecular genetics (MIMG), and neuroscience from UCLA. My transition into engineering was driven by a growing interest in designing, modeling, testing, and improving complex physical systems.

After taking my first controls course, I became particularly interested in Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC), flight dynamics, and systems engineering. I want to pursue a career in missile and defense systems because they integrate guidance, control, propulsion, sensing, and vehicle dynamics into a single performance system.

My goal is to apply my multidisciplinary background and engineering knowledge to the development of advanced aerospace and defense technologies that strengthen national security and protect the United States and its allies.
