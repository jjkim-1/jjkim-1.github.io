Flight Dynamics & Control

Nonlinear 6-DOF Flight Dynamics & LQR Control

Explore Full Project →

MATLAB Based Nonlinear Flight Dynamics, Linearization, and Closed-Loop Control

Developed a modular nonlinear 6-DOF aerospace vehicle simulation in MATLAB using quaternion attitude dynamics and rigid body equations of motion. Extended the model through steady flight trim, numerical linearization, modal and controllability analysis, and continuous time LQR control design. The controller was developed from a reduced state space model and applied to the original nonlinear 13 state vehicle simulation. Nonlinear closed-loop testing was used to evaluate disturbance rejection and controller performance.
