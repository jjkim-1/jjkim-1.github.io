01

6-DOF Flight Vehicle Simulation

Nonlinear Flight Dynamics and Control Simulation

Developed a six-degree-of-freedom flight vehicle simulation to model translational and rotational vehicle motion and provide a foundation for control-system development and analysis.

Technical Areas

MATLAB • Simulink • 6-DOF Dynamics • State-Space Modeling • Quaternions • Direction Cosine Matrices • Flight Dynamics

Project Objectives

  • Model nonlinear translational and rotational equations of motion.
  • Implement reference-frame transformations and vehicle attitude representation.
  • Establish trim conditions and linearize the nonlinear vehicle model around selected operating points.
  • Develop a simulation framework suitable for closed-loop control-system analysis.
